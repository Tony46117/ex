Our Story

Rooted in German
Tradition

Bremer House brings the finest ex-German furniture and antiques directly from Germany to your home. Sourced from the historic port city of Bremerhaven and surrounding regions, each piece represents decades of meticulous German craftsmanship.

From ornate Biedermeier cabinets to sturdy Bauhaus-inspired shelving, our collection spans centuries of European design philosophy. We personally select every item to ensure authenticity, quality, and timeless appeal.
